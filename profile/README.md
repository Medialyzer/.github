# Medialyzer

**Media Impact Analyzer.** Medialyzer measures what TV advertising does.

## Products

- **Tracking**: detects TV ads as they air and delivers the detections to customers.
- **Attribution**: lines TV airings up with app installs and website visits.
- **Lyzio**: an AI agent that helps plan TV advertising.
